# Product: Liaozhai Nights 聊齋夜話

- Interactive Chinese ghost-story website for Kiroween 2026, Costume Contest category.
- Inspired by Pu Songling's Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio (聊齋誌異).
- Player = a young scholar on the way to the imperial exams. An AI storyteller narrates each scene and offers 3 choices.
- Encounters: fox spirit, painted-skin demon, hopping jiangshi. At least 3 endings.
- Each ending is written as a short classical-Chinese-style tale.
- Learning angle (for real-world impact): tap any sentence to see modern Chinese + English. Audience: Chinese learners and students new to classical Chinese.
- Look and feel: old book with turning pages, rice-paper texture, candlelight flicker, red paper talismans, ink-brush fonts. Spooky but beautiful. Desktop and mobile.
