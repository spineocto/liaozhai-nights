# 📜 Liaozhai Nights 聊齋夜話

> An interactive Chinese ghost-story website, built with [Kiro](https://kiro.dev) for **Kiroween 2026** (Costume Contest).

Inspired by Pu Songling's *Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio* (聊齋誌異). You play a young scholar traveling to the imperial exams. An AI storyteller narrates each scene by candlelight, and your choices decide whether you meet a fox spirit 🦊, a painted-skin demon 👹, or a hopping jiangshi 🧟 — ending with your fate written as a short classical-Chinese tale.

## Why it matters
Classical Chinese is beautiful but hard. Liaozhai Nights turns it into a game: tap any sentence to see a plain modern-Chinese version and an English translation, so Chinese learners and students can enjoy the original style without getting lost.

## Features (planned)
- 🕯️ Old-book UI with turning pages, rice-paper texture, candlelight and paper talismans
- 🤖 AI storyteller with branching choices and at least 3 endings
- 📖 Tap-to-translate: classical → modern Chinese → English
- 📱 Works on desktop and phone
