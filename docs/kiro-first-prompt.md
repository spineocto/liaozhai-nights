# First prompt for Kiro (paste into Spec mode)

I want to build "Liaozhai Nights" (聊齋夜話), a web app for Kiroween 2026, Costume Contest category.

What it is:
- An interactive Chinese ghost-story website inspired by Pu Songling's Strange Tales from a Chinese Studio.
- The player is a young scholar traveling to the imperial exams. An AI storyteller narrates each scene and offers 3 choices.
- Choices lead to encounters with a fox spirit, a painted-skin demon, or a hopping jiangshi, and to one of at least 3 endings.
- The ending is written as a short classical-Chinese-style tale, with a modern Chinese and English explanation.

Who it's for and why it matters:
- Chinese learners and students who find classical Chinese hard: tapping any sentence shows a plain modern Chinese version and an English translation.

Look and feel:
- An old book with turning pages, rice-paper texture, candlelight flicker, red paper talismans, ink-brush fonts. Spooky but beautiful, works on desktop and phone.

Constraints:
- I'm a beginner, so keep the tech simple: a single web app that is easy to deploy for free (e.g. Vercel or Netlify).
- Keep API keys out of the browser code.
- Repo must be public with an MIT license, and the /.kiro folder must stay in the repo.

Please start by writing the requirements, design, and task list as a spec, and explain each step to me in simple words.
